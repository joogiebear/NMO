The piece that unlocks everything else

Four photo roles, four treatments.

The design has photo-shaped holes but no rules for them. These four roles say what each picture is for, how it's cropped, and what may be done to it — so the shots you gather this week drop straight in.

One hard rule

Never tint or duotone a person's face. Atmosphere can be styled; people are shown as they are.

Until they arrive

The placeholders stay honest — a labeled slot beats a stock photo of strangers, and it tells anyone editing the site exactly what belongs there. Landscape shots matter more than portrait ones: one good wide photo of the block party unlocks the hero band, the events page and the social card all at once.
