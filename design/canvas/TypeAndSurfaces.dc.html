Type and surfaces

One weight, one surface, all the way down.

Every heading on the site is Fraunces 600 and almost every block is a white card on a warm ground. It reads as competent and slightly generic. Two cheap fixes below — neither needs new photos.

Option A — recommended · use the range Fraunces already has

Trade-off: none to speak of — the font is already loaded, so this costs one line of CSS. Least disruptive, keeps the identity you have.

Trade-off: more distinctive and closer to a flyer, but colder — the warmth in the current design comes largely from that serif. Worth it only if you want the site to feel younger.

Stop making everything a white card
Paper + grain Default ground. Grain is the cheapest atmosphere there is.
Warm band Alternating sections, so the page has a pulse.
Plum Reserved for asks. Never twice in a row.
Halftone gold Events and merch only. Cheap-print energy, on purpose.

Four grounds in a fixed rotation replace the current one-note stack of white cards. The rule that keeps it disciplined: a section may change its ground or its layout, never both at once.
